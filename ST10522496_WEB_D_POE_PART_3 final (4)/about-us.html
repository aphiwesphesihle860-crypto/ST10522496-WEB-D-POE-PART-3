About Aura Luxe

A Durban fragrance house built on one idea: everyone deserves a signature scent that lasts.

Our Story - Brief History

Aura Luxe was founded in 2026 in Durban, KwaZulu-Natal with one simple idea: everyone deserves a signature scent that lasts.

We started on Instagram and WhatsApp, selling curated luxury fragrances to friends, students, and professionals who wanted authentic, long-lasting perfumes without paying R3000+ in malls.

Today we specialise in everything related to perfume — Eau de Parfum (EDP), Eau de Toilette (EDT), perfume oils, body mists, gift sets, atomizers, decants, storage cases, and scented candles.

We built our collection around 7 fragrance families so every customer can find their aura: Oud & Amber, Floral, Woody Musk, Fresh Aquatic, Citrus, Gourmand Sweet, and Spicy Leather. Shop the collection.
